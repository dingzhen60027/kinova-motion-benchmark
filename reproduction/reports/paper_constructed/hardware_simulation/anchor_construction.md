# Fixed-anchor construction before planning

The first actual preflight failed and was retained. Both declared configurations had a **4.1666 mm penetration between the public iiwa link 5 and link 7 collision boxes**. This was a geometric self-collision in the public model, not an online scene obstacle. No collision filters, collision boxes, or gripper transform were changed to remove it.

Before any edit, 32 source/configuration files were copied with SHA256 identities into `check_01/source_snapshot`. The complete initial signed-distance records are in `check_01/anchor_pair_diagnosis.json`. Two temporary-driver import failures and their correction are retained in `hardware_anchor_commands.json` and separate logs.

`construct_anchors.py` wrote its selection contract before evaluating any candidate. It enumerated 420 mirrored pairs from the finite Cartesian product of joint 1 magnitude, shoulder, elbow and wrist angles. The remaining joints were fixed at zero. Candidates were canonicalized through float32. It required TCP x in [0.2, 0.8] m, absolute y in [0.25, 0.8] m, z in [0.25, 0.9] m, the correct sign of y, valid joint limits, and collision freedom under the static and all 15 frozen voxel models. This workspace and ordering are explicit construction choices, not recovered author targets.

All 840 configurations received FK, joint-limit, workspace and 16 native collision checks. Forty-two candidate pairs passed these checks. Eligible pairs were considered in the preregistered product order. The first pair (index 0) passed independent signed-distance checks against the static model, all 15 voxel models and all 15 exact-solid scene models, with required clearance 1 mm and measured minimum **29.703995 mm**. Independent checks for later pairs were explicitly left untested after acceptance; they are not reported as passing. Native flags for every candidate remain saved.

The selected right/left configurations are `[-0.800000011920929, 0.6000000238418579, 0, -0.800000011920929, 0, 0, 0]` and `[0.800000011920929, 0.6000000238418579, 0, -0.800000011920929, 0, 0, 0]`. The right target-generating pose also defines the initial virtual state. The full target transforms are computed from these frozen configurations. No IK, roadmap, path planner, inflation or trajectory-optimization result was available or used during selection.

The construction completed in 0.866 s inside the driver (1.453 s whole command). The amended preflight explicitly checks all three declared anchors (initial, left and right), 256 random static-model queries, the first-layout predicate probe, and every anchor against all 15 voxel and exact-solid layouts. `check_02` actually passed, with zero native/independent disagreements, in 1.358 s whole-command time. These diagnostic timings are not planning benchmark timings.

Evidence is retained in `anchor_construction_01`: the preregistration, source snapshot, all candidate configurations and FK poses, all native predicates, candidate statuses, voxel centers and frozen scenes, independent pair names/distances, proposed configuration, application identity and terminal manifest. The selected anchors establish only geometric validity of the fixed task inputs; they do not establish roadmap connectivity, successful planning, continuous safety or physical performance. Later failures must preserve the same scenes and targets.
